# Cubby — ICP Analysis

> Phase 1 deliverable. Ideal Customer Profile for English-speaking families.

---

## Primary ICP: "The Organized Parent"

**Demographics:**
- Age 28-42, parent in a nuclear family with 1-3 children
- Tier 1 city (Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Dubai, Abu Dhabi)
- Household income: ₹15-50 LPA (India) or AED 15K-40K/mo (UAE)
- English-speaking, smartphone-savvy, uses 20+ apps daily
- Both parents working, or one parent managing household full-time

**Behavior:**
- Already digitizes some documents (photos in camera roll, PDFs in Google Drive)
- Uses WhatsApp as a "forwarding archive" for important documents
- Has a physical folder or drawer of critical papers at home
- Manages children's school/health appointments mentally or in Apple/Google Calendar
- Has experienced the pain of not finding a document when needed (at hospital, at school, at immigration)

**Pain triggers:**
- "Where is Aarav's vaccination card?" — 15 minutes searching WhatsApp, gallery, Google Drive
- "When does our car insurance expire?" — has to call the insurance company
- "Which doctor prescribed this medicine?" — can't remember, photo lost in chat
- "I need our property documents for the loan application" — drives home to get the physical folder
- "The school needs Ria's birth certificate AGAIN" — already submitted twice, never saved a copy

**Willingness to pay:** ₹100-200/mo for something that genuinely reduces mental load. Price-sensitive but values premium experiences (pays for Spotify, Netflix, gym membership).

**Acquisition channels:** Instagram ads targeting parents, parenting Facebook/WhatsApp groups, word-of-mouth from other parents, school parent WhatsApp groups.

---

## Secondary ICP: "The Digital-First Parent"

**Demographics:**
- Age 25-35, tech-savvy, early adopter
- Works in tech/finance/consulting
- Uses Notion, Obsidian, or similar productivity tools personally
- 1-2 young children (0-6 years)

**Behavior:**
- Already has a system (Google Drive folders, Notion databases) but frustrated with the manual effort
- Would immediately appreciate the AI classification and entity-linking
- Most likely to share with extended family (grandparents)
- Power user of the assistant feature

**Why they matter:** They're the early evangelists. They'll write App Store reviews, share on Twitter/LinkedIn, and convince their extended family to use it. They're also the most likely to provide quality feedback on AI accuracy.

---

## Tertiary ICP: "The Overwhelmed Manager"

**Demographics:**
- Age 35-50, one parent (usually mother) managing the household
- Tier 2 city (Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Lucknow, Kochi, Chandigarh)
- Household income: ₹8-20 LPA
- Moderate tech comfort, uses WhatsApp and Google Pay fluently

**Behavior:**
- Everything is in her head or in WhatsApp forwards
- No existing digital organization system
- Manages documents for children, spouse, parents, and sometimes in-laws
- Experiences the highest mental load and would benefit most from Cubby
- Least likely to discover the app organically (needs targeted marketing)

**Pain triggers:** Same as primary but more intense. Managing documents for 6-8 family members across generations. The mental load is the strongest selling point.

**Willingness to pay:** ₹50-100/mo. Price-sensitive. May prefer annual plan with discount. Lifetime plan could work well for this segment.

---

## Anti-personas (who Cubby is NOT for)

- **Enterprise users** looking for business document management (DMS+, EisenVault serve them)
- **Solo individuals** without family context (DigiLocker, Smarana serve them)
- **Families who don't own smartphones** (not technically feasible)
- **Families who exclusively use regional languages** with no English literacy (Phase 1 constraint)
- **Financial advisors/wealth managers** (Famli serves them, Cubby is not a fintech)

---

## Geographic priority

1. **India Tier 1** (Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai) — Largest pool of English-speaking, tech-savvy families
2. **UAE** (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah) — High-income Indian expat families, English-first, strong willingness to pay
3. **India Tier 2** (Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Kochi, Chandigarh) — Larger volume, lower ARPU
4. **Singapore, Southeast Asia** — Future expansion
